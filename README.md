# Awesome-Youtube-Community-Platforms

# Top YouTube Community Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Member Engagement, Discussion Forums & Community Networking*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **YouTube Community Platforms**. These tools help creators build and manage their own communities outside of YouTube's comment section — facilitating discussions, sharing exclusive content, and fostering deeper fan relationships through forums, chat, voice channels, and membership management.

**Examples** include Discord, Circle.so, Skool, Mighty Networks, Discourse, Geneva, Whop, Telegram, Hivebrite, and Bettermode (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom community workflows, and transparent member data — ideal for creators prioritizing data sovereignty, developers building community platforms, and audiences avoiding per-seat or percentage-based fees.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Discord](https://discord.com/)**  
  Leading community chat platform with text channels, voice rooms, video calls, and extensive bot ecosystem. Free tier is generous; Nitro adds higher upload limits and HD streaming .

- **[Circle.so](https://circle.so/)**  
  All-in-one community platform with courses, events, payments, and member management for creators and businesses. Percentage-based fees scale with member growth .

- **[Skool](https://skool.com/)**  
  Community platform with courses, gamification, and member management. Flat $99/month pricing with no platform fee beyond payment processing .

- **[Mighty Networks](https://www.mightynetworks.com/)**  
  Community platform with courses, events, and membership management for creators. Percentage fees scale with member growth .

- **[Discourse](https://www.discourse.org/)**  
  Open-source discussion platform available as managed hosting, with 200+ plugins covering chat, gamification, and SSO integration .

- **[Geneva](https://geneva.com/)**  
  Community platform with chat, voice, video, and events designed for creators and groups.

- **[Whop](https://whop.com/)**  
  Marketplace and community platform with payments, gated content, and member management.

- **[Telegram](https://telegram.org/)**  
  Messaging platform with groups, channels, and bots for community engagement.

- **[Hivebrite](https://hivebrite.com/)**  
  Community management platform for alumni networks, associations, and professional communities.

- **[Bettermode](https://bettermode.com/)**  
  Customizable community platform with spaces, discussions, and member engagement tools.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Roost](https://github.com/msrbuilds/roost)**  
  Self-hosted community platform positioned as an open-source Skool/Circle alternative with Stripe payments, learn mode, live rooms, and more. Features community feed, groups with roles, course modules with progress tracking, direct messaging, events with RSVP, leaderboard, member directory, showcase gallery, live streaming, notifications, and feature requests. React/TypeScript frontend with Express/Node.js backend, Supabase or MongoDB, and S3-compatible storage. Deploy via Docker, Dockploy, Vercel, or Netlify .

- **[Discourse](https://github.com/discourse/discourse)**  
  Leading open-source discussion platform built with Ruby on Rails, Ember.js, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Features 200+ plugins, trust levels, auto-moderation, full-text search, SSO support (SAML, LDAP, OAuth), and mailing list mode. ActivityPub plugin enables federation with the fediverse. GPL-2.0 licensed .

- **[Flarum](https://github.com/flarum/framework)**  
  Modern, fast, and extensible forum software built with PHP. Rated #1 "best web forum software" on Slant. MIT licensed with extension ecosystem and clean responsive design .

- **[NodeBB](https://github.com/NodeBB/NodeBB)**  
  Node.js-based forum software with real-time notifications via WebSockets, mobile responsiveness, and RESTful APIs. Native ActivityPub federation support. Supports Redis, MongoDB, or PostgreSQL. GPL-3.0 licensed .

- **[Bonfire](https://bonfirenetworks.org/)**  
  Open-source, self-hostable, and federated community platform. Provides groups, circles, events, and decisions that communities make together — going beyond forums to a fuller community shape. Social layer federates across the fediverse today; group federation in development with W3C task force .

- **[Storyden](https://github.com/southclaws/storyden)**  
  Modern forum platform combining discussion forums, curation system (Reddit/Pinterest-style), and wiki functionality. Prioritizes permanence and discoverability with auto-tagging, auto-organization, comprehensive API access, and containerized deployment. SQLite or PostgreSQL with S3 integration. MPL-2.0 licensed .

- **[CommonPub](https://github.com/commonpub/commonpub)**  
  Open ActivityPub federation protocol and package suite for self-hosted maker communities. 14 packages including schema, server, protocol, auth, UI components, editor, docs, explainer, learning, and theme studio. Nuxt 3 + Vue 3 frontend, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM, Meilisearch, and ActivityPub federation .

- **[Daccord](https://github.com/DaccordProject/daccord)**  
  Free, open-source chat and voice alternative to Discord — chat, voice, and video with native apps you control. Connect to your own Accord server, not someone else's cloud. Real-time messaging, multi-server support, voice/video/screen sharing via LiveKit/WebRTC, custom emoji, and native Flutter apps for all platforms. GPLv3 licensed with no ads, no tracking, no corporate lock-in .

- **[Nodyx](https://github.com/Pokled/Nodyx)**  
  Self-hosted community platform combining Forum + Chat + Voice + P2P + Canvas + Homepage Builder in one install. Single server, one community, forever. Indexed forum with canonical URLs, real-time chat, P2P voice channels, collaborative canvas, drag-and-drop homepage builder, extension SDK, games, and OctoGuard auto-moderation. Works on a Raspberry Pi behind a home router — no domain, no open ports, no cloud account. AGPL-3.0 licensed .

- **[Backspace](https://github.com/TheZwiss/backspace)**  
  Self-hosted, open-source Discord alternative with voice, HD video, screen share up to 4K/120fps, and server-to-server federation. TypeScript, AGPL-3.0 licensed. Features LiveKit for voice/video, HMAC-signed federation for cross-instance friendships and calls, Electron desktop app, PWA mobile support, and opt-in telemetry .

- **[Harmony Server](https://github.com/tartine-studio/harmony-server)**  
  Self-hostable Discord alternative built with Go. Single binary — download, run, done. Embedded SQLite database created automatically. Text channels with WebSocket messaging, voice channels with built-in SFU (Pion WebRTC), presence tracking, and zero-config by default. Scales with optional PostgreSQL and Redis. AGPL-3.0 licensed .

- **[Valour](https://github.com/Valour-Software/Valour)**  
  Open-source community platform built with .NET and Blazor. Communities are "planets" with chat channels, thread feeds, wikis, voice/video calls, roles, economy, and a "village" where members walk around and meet. Client supports tabs, splits, and multiple conversations. Self-hosting via Docker Compose with PostgreSQL, Redis, and Caddy. Federation hub and community node architecture .

- **[Lineweb Social](https://github.com/drewmt/lineweb-social)**  
  Self-hosted social platform with spaces, stories, threaded conversations, polls, galleries, and a composer without pressure. Full social experience with light/dark mode, shareable search URLs, 24-hour stories without surveillance mechanics, and invite systems for cohort onboarding .

- **[Forem](https://github.com/forem/forem)**  
  Open-source community platform powering dev.to. Features posts, comments, tags, reactions, and user profiles. Free and open-source. 22.8k+ GitHub stars .

- **[Crikket](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source platform for building and managing creator memberships, digital products, communities, and subscription-based businesses. Provides authentication, payments, media storage, member management, and modern web interface. Self-hostable alternative to Patreon, Gumroad, or Circle .

- **[MemberMatters](https://github.com/membermatters/MemberMatters)**  
  Open-source membership, billing, and access control solution for makerspaces and community groups. Recurring membership payments via Stripe, member registration, self-service billing, SSO provider support (OIDC and Discourse protocol), audit logging, and access control firmware integration. Production quality for 5+ years .

- **[Createon](https://github.com/opd-ai/createon)**  
  Self-hosted Patreon alternative enabling creators to monetize content using cryptocurrency payments (Bitcoin and Monero). Multiple subscription tiers, Markdown content support, tier-restricted access, automated payment verification, and flat-file architecture with no database required. Go-based with CLI for creator management .

- **[Ashley](https://github.com/openfun/ashley)**  
  Self-hosted multi-tenant discussion forum for learning. Python/Django-based, MIT licensed .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Lemmy](https://github.com/LemmyNet/lemmy)** — Federated link aggregator and forum platform in Rust with ActivityPub support. Interoperates with Mastodon and other fediverse platforms. AGPL licensed .
- **[Mattermost](https://github.com/mattermost/mattermost)** — Open-source team communication and community messaging platform with omnichannel live chat integrations .
- **[Rocket.Chat](https://github.com/RocketChat/Rocket.Chat)** — Free, unlimited open-source team chat platform. Replace email, HipChat, and Slack with a self-hosted solution .
- **[Matrix / Element](https://github.com/vector-im/element-web)** — Open standard for secure, decentralized, real-time communication with bridges to Discord, Slack, Telegram, and IRC .
- **Fediverse Tools** — Mastodon, Pleroma, and other ActivityPub platforms for federated community engagement.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Discourse** or **NodeBB** for forum discussions, **Daccord** or **Backspace** for Discord-style chat and voice, **Roost** or **Circle-style community** for creator memberships, **Bonfire** for federated community groups, and **Nodyx** for all-in-one community on home hardware to build a complete self-hosted YouTube community platform stack.
